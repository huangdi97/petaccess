# COEXISTENCE_BOUNDARY_SPEC.md

## 目标
把“我不希望和普通宠物共食”等需求转成中性、结构化、可验证的空间边界。

## 场所规则/属性
首批：
- indoor dining access
- outdoor dining access
- animal on customer seat
- animal on table surface
- animal near food service area
- animal in self-service food area
- animal use customer tableware
- dedicated pet tableware
- dedicated pet zone
- zone separation

## 三者永久分离
1. Rule：场所规定
2. Observation：用户看到
3. Boundary：用户自己的接受边界
